Syed Zahid Ali Akhter

Zahid is the Principal and founder of the firm Zahid Architects, a landscape and architecture design consultancy firm established in 2003. He has over 15 years of professional and teaching experience. He started his landscape career working under renowned landscape architect, Prof. Shaheer, at New Delhi.

He obtained his Master of Landscape Architecture from the School of Planning and Architecture, Deemed University, New Delhi in 2002, and his Bachelor of Architecture from Bangalore University in 1999. He is a registered member of the Council of Architecture and an Associate member of the Indian Society of Landscape Architects (ISOLA), the South Asia Pacific Regional Chapter of IFLA.

Zahid has wide experience in handling diverse landscape projects, having worked across housing, apartment complexes, office complexes and institutions, down to smaller residences.

He has been Visiting Faculty at the Architecture and Planning Departments of JNAFAU, Hyderabad, and a Design Critic for SV College of Architecture, Hyderabad.

Featured in the Journal of Landscape Architecture, New Delhi, in the 2009 issue as an Emerging Landscape Architect.
