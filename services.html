What We Do

Our Services

Specialised consulting services from design brief through to construction completion.

Zahid Architects is a Landscape and Architectural Design firm. It was established in 2003 in Hyderabad, India. Having an experience of handling prestigious projects, Zahid Architects seeks to establish itself as one of the best professional offices in the field.

Zahid Architects works as a team with the Client, the Consultants and the Engineers from receiving the design brief of the project through its construction completion. We render specialised consulting services in the following areas:

  • Landscape Architecture
  • Architecture
  • Interiors
  • Planning

Methodology

We proceed by receiving the design brief of the project from the client, investigating the site’s opportunities and constraints, as well as exploring locally available materials and artefacts, and prepare a schematic design.

After taking necessary feedback and approvals from all concerned, we proceed to prepare the final concept plan. We then work on the design development of each space and its details. After final approval, we issue good-for-construction drawings.

We always remain intimately involved in the decision-making process, regardless of the size of the project or its phase of realisation.
