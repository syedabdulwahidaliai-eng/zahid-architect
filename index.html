About the Firm

A Landscape & Architectural Design practice built on collaboration.

Zahid Architects is a Landscape and Architectural Design firm established in 2003 in Hyderabad, India. Having experience of handling prestigious projects, Zahid Architects seeks to establish itself as one of the best professional offices in the field.

We work as a team with the client, the consultants and the engineers — from receiving the design brief of a project through to its construction completion — rendering specialised consulting services across landscape architecture, architecture, interiors and planning.

  • 01 Landscape Architecture & site planning
  • 02 Architecture, Interiors & Planning
  • 03 Close collaboration with clients, consultants & engineers
